# Liquidity Risk & Basel LCR Policy

*Internal Policy Manual — Treasury & Liquidity Risk*

## LCR-1. Liquidity Coverage Ratio Definition

The Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) is defined as the stock of High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)
divided by total net cash outflows over a 30-calendar-day stress period. The Bank's internal
minimum threshold is **100%**, consistent with Basel III liquidity standards.

## LCR-2. HQLA Composition

HQLA is composed of Level 1 assets (cash, central bank reserves, certain sovereign debt — no
haircut) and Level 2 assets (investment-grade corporate debt, certain covered bonds — subject to
15–50% haircuts), with Level 2 assets capped at 40% of total HQLA.

## LCR-3. Reporting Cadence

LCR must be calculated and reported to the Asset & Liability Committee (ALCO) monthly, and daily
during any period where the ratio falls within 10 percentage points of the internal minimum
threshold.

## LCR-4. Breach Escalation

Any daily LCR reading below 100% must be escalated to the CRO and Treasurer within 24 hours, with
a documented remediation plan and expected time-to-cure.
