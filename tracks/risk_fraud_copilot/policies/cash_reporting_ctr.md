# Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) Policy

*Internal Policy Manual — Financial Crime Compliance*
*Document: CTR · Version 2.0 · Owner: Head of Financial Crime Compliance*

> **Synthetic demo document**, modeled loosely on common cash-reporting practice for
> illustrative purposes only. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## CTR-1. Reporting Threshold

A Currency Transaction Report must be prepared for any single cash transaction, or multiple cash
transactions by or on behalf of the same customer that aggregate, to more than **$10,000** in a
single business day.

## CTR-2. Aggregation Rule

All cash transactions across all accounts held or controlled by the same customer at the Bank
must be aggregated for purposes of the CTR-1 threshold, including deposits and withdrawals
conducted at different branches or channels on the same business day.

## CTR-3. Filing Deadline

Completed CTRs must be filed within 15 calendar days of the reportable transaction.

## CTR-4. Relationship to Structuring Detection

Patterns of deliberately sub-threshold transactions designed to avoid the CTR-1 threshold are
addressed separately under `aml_transaction_monitoring.md`, section AML-TM-2 (Structuring /
Smurfing Detection), and require SAR — not CTR — handling.
