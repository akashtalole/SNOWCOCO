## KYC-RR-1. Risk Rating Methodology

Every customer is assigned a risk rating of **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** at onboarding and
re-assessed at least annually (High: every 12 months, Medium: every 24 months, Low: every 36
months). The rating is derived from customer type (individual vs. business), geographic exposure,
industry/occupation, product and channel usage, and expected transaction profile.

## KYC-RR-2. High-Risk Customer Indicators

A customer is rated **High** if any of the following apply: (a) business in a cash-intensive
industry (e.g., money services business, casino, precious metals dealer); (b) beneficial owner or
counterparty exposure to a Designated High-Risk Jurisdiction; (c) politically exposed person (PEP)
status; (d) prior SAR filings on the customer within the last 24 months.

## KYC-RR-3. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Triggers

EDD is required at onboarding for all High-risk customers, and post-onboarding whenever: a
Tier 2+ transaction monitoring alert is generated on the account (see `aml_transaction_monitoring.md`,
AML-TM-3, AML-TM-4), the customer's actual transaction volume materially exceeds their stated
expected activity, or adverse media is identified. EDD includes source-of-funds verification and
a documented analyst review within 10 business days.

## KYC-RR-4. Periodic Review and Escalation

Analysts performing periodic reviews must re-score the customer's risk rating using current
transaction history. Any change from Medium/Low to High must be logged with a Finding referencing
the transaction evidence that drove the change.
